# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge
We pledge to make participation in the CostScope project a harassment‑free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio‑economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our Standards
Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:
- Demonstrating empathy and kindness
- Using inclusive, professional language
- Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences
- Focusing on constructive feedback
- Showing generosity in reviews and mentorship

Unacceptable behavior includes:
- Sexualized language or imagery; unwelcome advances
- Trolling, insulting/derogatory comments, personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information without consent
- Other conduct reasonably considered inappropriate

## Enforcement Responsibilities
Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing standards. They may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and other contributions not aligned with this Code, and will communicate reasons for moderation decisions when appropriate.

## Scope
This Code applies within all project spaces and when representing the project in public spaces.

## Enforcement
Report abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior to: dev404ai@gmail.com (or the current address listed in SECURITY.md). Reports will be reviewed and responded to within 72 hours. A maintainer outside the direct area of concern will handle conflict of interest cases.

## Enforcement Guidelines
1. Correction – Notice of violation; expected change.
2. Warning – Formal warning and temporary exclusion from certain interactions.
3. Temporary Ban – Time‑bound exclusion from project spaces.
4. Permanent Ban – Removal from all public project spaces.

The maintainers will follow a fair process, ensuring confidentiality of reporters.
